The second Tripoli prefecture ever formed, and the first in Texas

Tripoli Houston was chartered on December 10, 1985. Forty years later we still meet every month to fly, from first-timers with a model rocket kit to members pushing the limits of our FAA waiver.

We added NAR Section 916 in 2022 and support both the Tripoli Rocketry Association and the National Association of Rocketry. Every newcomer gets a warm welcome. Bring kolaches and it gets warmer.

On launch day

  • Flying runs 9 am to 4 pm.
  • Both fields have restrooms, and hand sanitizer is on hand.
  • It gets hot. Bring water, sunscreen and your own shade.
  • Kids are welcome. They stay in your setup area, and a parent or guardian goes out with them to fly.
  • Read the launch site rules before your first visit.
  • Check the weather links the morning of.

Fly with us

Before your rocket goes on the rail, take care of these three things.

  1. Be a TRA or NAR member

    Join the Tripoli Rocketry Association or the National Association of Rocketry. Under 18, or not a member? Have a signed TRA waiver of release on file. We fly under TRA insurance.

    Join TRAJoin NAR

  2. Register for the launch

    Registration for each monthly launch opens one week before launch day. South Site launches always need registration.

    Open registration

  3. Bring your flight card

    Print your flight card and any member forms before you head out, so you can fly as soon as you get to the field.

    Flight cards and forms
